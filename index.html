By translating your English product name into Chinese industrial belt terminology (such as 源头工厂 or 超级工厂), US Amazon FBA and TikTok Shop sellers unlock true tier-1 EXW RMB pricing at 100–200 unit MOQs.

2. 4 Chinese Characters That Expose Trading Middlemen

Before paying for samples, inspect the supplier's 1688 business license scope (经营范围). Verified manufacturers explicitly list 生产 (Production), 制造 (Manufacturing), or 加工 (Processing). Resellers only list 批发 (Wholesale) or 贸易 (Trading). Combine this with industrial cluster alignment (e.g., Ningbo for injection plastics, Shenzhen for 3C electronics).
