1. Check the Business License Scope (经营范围) for 3 Exact Chinese Words

Click the supplier's corporate archive badge (企业档案) on 1688 and inspect the 经营范围 (Business Scope) field. A genuine manufacturer must legally list 生产 (Production), 制造 (Manufacturing), or 加工 (Processing). If the license only lists 批发 (Wholesale), 零售 (Retail), 贸易 (Trading), or 电子商务 (E-commerce), they are a middleman flipping another factory's goods.
